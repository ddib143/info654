About Miranda, founder of Art 4 Kidz:

Miranda Twombley, an art educator for 15 years, currently teaches art at the Johnson School in New York City for grades kindergarten through 8th grade. She started the Art 4 Kidz initiative during the pandemic to get kids excited about art.
